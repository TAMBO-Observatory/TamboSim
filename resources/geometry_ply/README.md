
`terrain_corsika.ply` = a full mesh of the entire earth's terrain, with increased detail near the Colca Canyon / specifically near the Valley of the Volcanoes.

`injection_region_corsika.ply` = a small mesh spanning the western side of the Valley of the Volcanoes.

`sphere_earth.ply` = a perfect sphere with radius = 6,371km (i.e. the Earth radius constant used by CORSIKA and Tambo.jl)

`tambo_inclined_plane.ply` = an inclined plane centered at the western side of the Valley of the Volcanoes with about 30deg incline with respect to the horizontal. the normal vector points upwards. 

`tambo_flat_plane_at_3.0km.ply` = a flat plane centered at the western side of the Valley of the Volcanoes, located at 3km elevation. the normal vector points upwards.
